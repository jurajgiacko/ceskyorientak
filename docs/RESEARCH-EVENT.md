## 1. Identity of the event

- **Official name:** Orienteering World Cup 2026, **Round 3** (`B4` §1). The IOF
  season has 12 competitions across 4 rounds: R1 Switzerland (sprint, 24–26 Apr),
  R2 Sweden (sprint, 28–31 May), **R3 Czechia (forest, 6–9 Aug)**, R4 Lithuania
  (24–26 Sep). (`B1-2` p5)
- **Organiser:** Czech Orienteering Federation (Český svaz orientačních sportů,
  ČSOS), Zátopkova 100/2, Praha 6 – Břevnov, 169 00. (`B4` §1.4)
- **Dates:** 4–9 August 2026 (competition 5–9 August).
- **Website:** wcup.cz · **Eventor:** eventor.orienteering.sport/Events/Show/8245
- **Round 3 is a purely forest round** — the World Cup itself has no sprint. The
  only sprint of the week is the *public* GAPP Czech O-Tour Prologue in Český
  Krumlov (see §6).

### 3.6 Terrain summary — the one-line version
Every World Cup race shares the same signature: **submountainous granite forest,
700–1,000 m a.s.l., "rocky towns" (skalní města) and stony ground, patches of
marsh, a good vehicle-track network but sparse paths, runnability swinging from
very good to impenetrable green — and the ruins of villages emptied after 1945.**

**Czech wording (o-tour.cz, for the same forest):**
> *"Podhorský les se skalními městy a kamenitými pasážemi, v některých částech
> bažinaté oblasti, poměrně hustá síť cest, množství zídek a hrázek, pozůstatky
> vysídlených vesnic. Průběžnost lesa proměnlivá (velmi dobrá až téměř
> neprůstupná). Kopcovitý terén s mnoha vrcholy, nadmořská výška 610 – 890 m.n.m."*

Note the Czech public-race text adds two features the English WC text omits:
**"množství zídek a hrázek"** — *many low stone walls and small dams/banks*.
These are the field boundaries of the vanished German villages.

## 4. Arena Martínkov — layout and access (`B4` §3.3)

- Meadow near the **former village of Martínkov**. GPS **48.6008 N, 14.2913 E**.
- **Parking** on a meadow within **400 m** of the arena, **shared with
  spectators**; the **team area is the western part**. Teams park free with a
  team parking card.
- **Access is one-way (clockwise).**
  - **In:** from Vyšší Brod south on Road 161 to **Studánky**, then right onto a
    smaller road to the arena parking (6 km / 10 min from the Event Centre).
  - **Out:** continue west, right at the only crossing, on toward Vyšší Brod
    (4 km / 6 min). The exit road is two-way for cyclists — care needed.
- **No indoor facilities** in the after-finish team zone — **tents only**.
- Teams may not enter the arena before their race; anyone who has been in the
  arena may not go to quarantine / start / pre-start.
- **Spectator control** is the visual centrepiece: it is the **last control**,
  punched as **control 100**, and runners punch it **twice** (arena passage then
  finish loop). It sits at a **black cross = exposed car**.
- **Event Centre (Office):** Info Centre building, **main square of Vyšší Brod**,
  48.6146 N, 14.3118 E.
- **Quarantines:** Qualification & Relay 100–200 m west of arena parking (no
  climb); Long & Middle 1,800 m from arena parking (no significant climb), route
  marked with red ribbons, no GPS given.
- **Prize giving & concerts:** **Monastery Gardens, Vyšší Brod** (not the arena).
- **Ticketing:** spectators buy via **dohlas.se/csos/owc26** in advance; wristband
  valid for the whole World Cup or a single day; parking and shuttle bus also
  sold there. **O-Tour entrants get the arena ticket free.**

## 5. Embargoed areas (`B4` §7)

- Per **IOF Competition Rules § 26.5**. All embargoed areas are under **strict**
  embargo — forbidden to any potential OWC R3 athlete, team official, or anyone
  whose terrain knowledge could influence results.
- Maps: `wcup.cz` and
  `eventor.orienteering.org/Documents/Event/8452/1/Embargoed-areas`
- **Embargo lifted from 5 August** for the roads serving as arena-parking access.
- On each race day, lifted for marked routes to quarantine/start, the quarantine
  itself, and designated warm-up areas — but entering the competition area
  outside one's own race time stays prohibited.
- Racing the GAPP Czech O-Tour is **not** an embargo violation; nor is playing
  the Venku.app Martínkov locality (after one's own race).
- **Previous orienteering maps of the area:** *Martínkov 2* (1998),
  *Mnichovice* (1998).

## 9. Vyšší Brod, the Vltava and the Šumava/Lipno landscape

### 9.1 The town (organisers' words, `B1-2` §3.1)
> *"Dubbed a 'treasury of cultural and natural beauty', this small town located
> on the banks of the River Vltava is the starting point for thousands of
> paddlers heading down the stream via Rožmberk, Zlatá Koruna and Český Krumlov,
> UNESCO World Heritage Site. **Above Vyšší Brod there are beautiful forests with
> many rocks and stones.**"*

Deputy Mayor **Jaroslav Marek** (`B1-2` p3):
> *"our beautiful South Bohemian border town, where history and the hustle and
> bustle of everyday life merge. Vyšší Brod is one of the pearls of South
> Bohemia, not only thanks to our **Cistercian monastery**, but also thanks to
> the flawless natural scenery…"*

**Concrete facts for art direction**
- **Cistercian monastery (Klášter Vyšší Brod)**, klastervyssibrod.cz — an
  Institutional Partner of the event. Its **Monastery Gardens host every prize
  giving and both evening concerts.** This is the event's ceremonial venue and
  the strongest architectural motif available.
- **Town square** (náměstí) with the **Info Centre = Event Centre**, 48.6146 N,
  14.3118 E — hosts the Opening Ceremony and the technical model.
- **River Vltava** runs through the town; it is the put-in point for the classic
  Vltava paddling route (Vyšší Brod → Rožmberk → Zlatá Koruna → Český Krumlov).
- **Town elevation ≈ 560 m a.s.l.**; the **race forest sits at 700–1,000 m**,
  i.e. the terrain is on the plateau *above* the town — matching the bulletin's
  "above Vyšší Brod there are beautiful forests with many rocks and stones".
  *(The 560 m town figure is general geographic knowledge, not from a bulletin —
  verify before publishing.)*

### 9.2 Lipno & Šumava (organisers' words, `B1-2` §3.1)
> *"Sometimes called the 'Czech sea', Lake Lipno is set within the fascinating
> Šumava countryside below the ridges lining the boundary with Austria and
> Germany. With its length of 42 km and the maximum width of 10 km… The upper
> part of Lipno territory is situated in the **Šumava National Park** where you
> can hike up to the summits of around **1,300 metres**."*

- Lipno nad Vltavou is **15 minutes' drive** from Vyšší Brod (`B4` §16).

### 9.3 Landscape character — what the terrain actually looks like
Assembled from the bulletins' own terrain wording (§3 above):
- **Granite bedrock** expressed as **"rocky towns" / "skalní města"** — clustered
  tors and boulder groups — plus continuous **stony ground** that measurably
  slows runnability.
- **Spruce-dominated submountainous forest**, runnability from very good to
  **"impenetrable green"**, with **chaotic vegetation** in places and localised
  **thickets** and **undergrowth**.
- **Marshes** in some parts.
- **Good vehicle-track network, sparse and irregular path network.**
- **Human traces:** **remains of abandoned villages**, **many low stone walls and
  small dams ("zídek a hrázek")**, and — from the Sport Director's foreword —
  **traces of the Iron Curtain border zone**. An **exposed car wreck** in the
  forest is significant enough to carry the spectator control (control 100).
- **Relief:** hilly with many summits, 610–1,000 m across all areas.

### 9.4 The historical layer — the event's core narrative
**Radim Hošek, Sport Director, Bulletin 4 p2 — quote this, it is the event's
own framing:**
> *"The sprint season is over, let us return to the forest. Around Vyšší Brod,
> wild and rugged terrains provide the perfect playground for world-class
> orienteering. Their character is not only a gift of nature, but also the result
> of moving chapters of Czech history. A century ago, much of this landscape was
> farmland and villages inhabited mainly by the German minority. After WW2, the
> population was expelled, the villages disappeared, and the area later became
> part of the Iron Curtain border zone. Traces of the former settlements and the
> Iron Curtain can still be found both on the map and in the terrain. For one
> week, orienteering will bring new life to this landscape, celebrating mutual
> respect and fair play over rivalry. Although all four races share the same
> arena, each terrain offers a distinct challenge. We aimed to make the most of
> each terrain while keeping the courses fair, challenging and enjoyable.
> Welcome back to the forest. Welcome to South Bohemia. Welcome to Vyšší Brod."*

**Jan Picek, Event Director (`B1-2` p2):** *"Czechia calls!"* — and the stated
ambition to *"build on the success of 2023, when together with you we created a
truly phenomenal orienteering festival."*
